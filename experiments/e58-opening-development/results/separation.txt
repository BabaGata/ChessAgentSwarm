DO THE SIGNALS SEPARATE A 2600 FROM A 1600?
======================================================================

strong players scored   100
subject players scored  72

Each player's own rate, both judged against the SAME strong-player
expectation. AUC is the chance a random subject scores worse than a
random strong player: 0.50 is a coin, 1.00 is perfect separation.

signal                    strong   subject   above p75      AUC
---------------------------------------------------------------
slow development             33%       48%         64%     0.76
late castling                29%       42%         68%     0.73
pawn share                   38%       34%         17%     0.25
repeat share                 31%       36%         58%     0.70

======================================================================
DOES slow_development CARRY ANYTHING late_castling DOES NOT?
======================================================================

`ready_at` is the later of castling and development, so the two
overlap by construction. If they correlate above 0.85 they are one
signal with two names and only the stronger ships -- the ceiling the
design states, and the screen E10 established.

  strong     r =  0.57   rates within 5 pp of each other for 35% of players
  subject    r =  0.63   rates within 5 pp of each other for 25% of players
  pooled     r =  0.68

  subjects whose slow rate exceeds their late rate by 15+ pp: 24/72
  (these are players who castle on time and still leave a piece at home)

A signal near AUC 0.50 fires equally on both populations and cannot
carry a claim, however sensible it sounds. That is what closed a
section slot in E10.
