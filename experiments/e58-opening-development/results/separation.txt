DO THE SIGNALS SEPARATE A 2600 FROM A 1600?
======================================================================

strong players scored   100
subject players scored  72

Each player's own rate, both judged against the SAME strong-player
expectation. AUC is the chance a random subject scores worse than a
random strong player: 0.50 is a coin, 1.00 is perfect separation.

signal                    strong   subject   above p75      AUC
---------------------------------------------------------------
late castling                29%       42%         68%     0.73
pawn share                   38%       34%         17%     0.25
repeat share                 31%       36%         58%     0.70

A signal near AUC 0.50 fires equally on both populations and cannot
carry a claim, however sensible it sounds. That is what closed a
section slot in E10.
