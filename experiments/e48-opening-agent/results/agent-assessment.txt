AGENT ASSESSMENT OF THE GUIDE LINKS — a first pass, not an endorsement
==============================================================================

This is the agent's reading, recorded so the author's pass is a confirm-or-
override rather than a cold read of ninety links. NOTHING here is marked
reviewed=true in the library: that flag means the author endorsed it, and it
still does.

Two levels of evidence, and they are kept apart:

  READ    the page text was actually read
  METRIC  only the counts were seen — treat as triage, not judgement

THE SCREEN HAS A CONFOUND, FOUND BY READING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The PLAN metric counts forward-looking second-person language. Marketing uses
the same grammar. Two of the three highest scores are inflated by it:

  Scandinavian / Chueca      plan 8.7  — but one match is "If you are finding
                             how to improve your chess level, I recommend you
                             to check The Chueca Method"
  London / TheChessWorld     plan 5.3  — matches are meta, not plans:
                             "How do you study the London System?",
                             "You want to understand the plans"

So the screen is a reliable filter for what is BAD — thin, storefront,
jargon-dense — and an unreliable ranking of what is GOOD. Every recommendation
below that rests on the metric alone says so.

WHAT THIS PASS CANNOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

stays yours.
