`ratio` near 1.00 means the setting finds no more clustering than a
player's own error rate produces on its own -- it would be measuring
how often they err, which S3 already reports, and not whether the
errors come one after another.

games. Small is good: it is the chance of seeing this much clustering
if the drops were independent.
