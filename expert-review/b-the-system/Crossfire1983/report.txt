WHAT TO DO

1. In the cited positions, count your opponent's pieces that can reach your king and compare it with your own defenders. The move to find is the one before they got a third attacker there — that is where the game turned, not the move the attack landed on.
   Why    seen in 12 of 20 games, at 4.9% against 2.0% for peers at your level
   Start  Do this one first — the others can wait their turn.

2. After choosing a move and before playing it, check every pawn you own that no longer has a defender. Dropped pawns rarely lose a game on their own and they are what your mistakes are most often costing you.
