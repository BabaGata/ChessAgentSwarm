  Anything it said that is simply wrong?
Examples of forks are not correct. Fork is not just having attacked just random piece and a pawn. Nor having 2 different pieces attacked by 2 different pieces. The loss of material in those examples happened just because the player lef pieces hanging. Forks occur when a less worth piece attackes 2 higher worth pieces simultaniously. Like a pawn attacking 2 knights simultaniously after moving it. Or knight attacking queen adn rock simultanuiously after being moved. Also for is when 2 undefendent pieces are attacked simultaniously by any piece and they cannot be defended both.
For example game Odqpej9W, move 7, you played c6d4 (e5e4 was better) this did not happened in move 7 but in move 6.
For example game QjeRmzq0, move 18, you played e7b4 (c6b4 was better) this also happened the move before.
It seems that for black you write the number of move one move later than it happened.
For example game QjeRmzq0, move 18, you played e7b4 (c6b4 was better) this example show a placing piece on the attacked spot and not just plain hanging piece. Hanging piece happens when the attacked piece was not moved nor defendend since it was attacked. If the piece moved on the attacked spot even if it was attacked before then it should be counted as placing a piece on the attacked spot.
