note that can be checked against a specific position and one that can only be
compared against a whole-game rate. Measured on the first player who did this:
the notes with a move number produced 20 checkable comparisons, the ones
without produced none.

worth writing down is a real answer — leave it out.

*Did the system see this mistake at all?* is a different question from *did it
choose to tell the player about it?*, and only the first is about whether the
analysis works.

   Optional, and unusually useful. "Hanging pieces" might mean undefended
