# Open Questions

The project's live register of unknowns. [[state]] says where we *are*; this note says what we
*don't know* and how each unknown gets closed.

**Rules.** Every entry has a stable ID (referenced from commits and other notes), an owner, what it
blocks, and a concrete resolution path — "think about it more" is not a resolution path. Entries are
never deleted; they are marked `resolved` with a link to where the answer lives. New questions get
appended with the next free ID in their section.

**Status:** `open` · `in-progress` · `resolved` · `deferred` (with a reason and a revisit trigger)
**Owner:** `author` = thesis author's decision · `agent` = resolvable by doing the work

| **D14** | **`NO_CHANGE_RATIO` is fitted for an error floor the analysis no longer uses** *(new 2026-08-16)* | **open — and flagged in the output** | agent | V7, and every "met / not met" verdict | [[experiments.e33-error-threshold]] lowered `INACCURACY_WP` 10.0 → 5.0. `NO_CHANGE_RATIO = 0.58` was fitted at 10.0 from 57 predictions across 84 players, cross-validated at 2 and 5 folds ([[experiments.e05-natural-drift]]), and a different floor means different rates, different regression, different constant. **Not hidden:** `labels.NO_CHANGE_RATIO_FITTED_AT_WP` records the floor it was fitted at, `calibration_is_stale()` compares it with the floor in use, and while they disagree the plan withdraws the "15–23 % reach this anyway" figure and states why. | **D13** | **The swarm names what punished a player and never why they allowed it** *(new 2026-08-16)* | open | agent+author | V2, V8, and the usefulness of every tactical finding | Raised by the reviewer mid-review: *"does the system evaluate the reason for why the player is losing pawns/pieces — leaving them undefended, moving them to an attacked square, calculating the exchange badly, not seeing defending moves, not seeing material-losing sequences? This kind of information seems more valuable for the advice than just saying that the player is losing the material."* **They are right, and the answer is no.** `gap_type` exists and is `UNKNOWN` unless a probe asked (D10), S2 supplies a *process* why (clock state) and nothing else does. The tactical motifs answer *what pattern punished you*, which is a different question. **Several of the causes they name look computable from what is already fetched** — undefended own pieces per position, whether a move's destination is attacked and undefended, static exchange evaluation on captures, whether an attacked piece was defended or moved — none needing an extra engine call. That makes this a **candidate section rather than a limitation**, and the screening question is E09/E11's: does any of it distinguish players once general error rate is divided out (L-025)? Until screened, the honest position is recorded in `expert-review/what-the-system-looks-for.txt` under "what it is blind to". |
| **D11** | **The endgame is largely undiagnosable under the current eligibility rule** *(new 2026-08-05)* | open | agent | S3, and any later endgame work | Building S3 found that **3 of 24 games** for a real player reached an endgame still inside `DECIDED_CP`. Structural, not tuning: `diagnosable()`'s window exists because win-probability labels compress at the extremes (L-009), and the endgame is precisely where games are most often already past it. **A rule designed for the middlegame removes most of the endgame.** Two routes, neither cheap: an error label for endgames that does not depend on win-probability deltas (e.g. distance-to-result, or tablebase-exact for ≤7 pieces — free and public), or accepting that this project can *describe* the endgame and not *diagnose* it, and saying so. Until then S3 speaks through its pooled claim and its advantage-retention claim, both of which work. |
| **D10** | **The prober's ground truth is written and labelled by its author** *(new 2026-08-04)* | **open — but no longer blocking** | agent+author | V2, V9, and any use of the prober on a real player | → [[experiments.e07-reason-classification]]. The classifier question is settled (`llama3.1:8b-instruct-q6_K`, kappa 0.74, zero false-ignorance). What is weak is the *labels*: 46 answers I invented, labelled by me, with the 0.74 measured in-sample because the refusal split came after seeing which items failed. **A better model cannot fix any of that.** Resolution path: ~40 answers collected from people who are not the author — a position, "what would you play and why", free text — and labelled by someone else. Cheap, unglamorous, and nothing downstream can be believed without it. Also the first real test of `declines_to_answer()`, which is a fixed phrase list and will miss what it has not seen. **Decision 2026-08-06: the gate is opened in `cli coach`, and closed by default in the standalone `probe` command.** A prober whose results cannot reach the diagnosis makes V9 demonstrable and inert, and the goal is a complete system. What replaces the gate is **provenance in the output**: the report states that a gap type came from *n* questions read by a classifier that agrees with its own rubric about three times in four, so the reader can weigh it. Honest and complete, rather than honest and disconnected — but the underlying weakness is unchanged and this question stays open. |
| **D9** | ~~**The no-change constant is depth-dependent and the planner applies one value**~~ **RESOLVED 2026-08-03** → [[experiments.e05-natural-drift]] § D9. Same 84 players, outcome period held whole, measurement period capped at 30/45/60/78 games. The dependence is **real, monotone and modest**: the fitted constant runs **0.488 → 0.594**, and with a single 0.58 the share met by drift runs **23 / 22 / 18 / 15 %**. **Two corrections to L-019 fell out:** 0.34 was never a fitted value at any depth (it was a hand-tightened guess from 13 predictions — the fit at that depth is 0.488), and drift at K=30 is +7.4 %, not the +11.2 % attributed to thinness. **Resolution:** no per-depth constant — four points do not determine a curve and fitting one is L-018's overfitting. The output states the measured **range (15–23 %)** instead of a point, so the thin-history penalty is visible to the player rather than hidden. | resolved | agent | — | — |
